  "Not enough information", pooled over well-formed rule predictions:

    full note       29.7%       14.7%
    masked (B6)     44.1%       13.4%

  Reading: masking removes information as intended (inclusion NEI 29.7% -> 44.1%), but the
  masked ranking scores HIGHER (0.817 vs 0.779), the opposite of the expected B6 drop. With 5
  patients and <= 6 trials each this is noise (one patient moves 0.72 -> 0.97); it is not a result.

Caveats - must be stated with these numbers
  * With at most 6 trials per patient, P@10 is identical for every ordering (all trials are in
    the top 10), a random ordering already scores NDCG@10 ~0.79, and 5
    patients cannot separate TrialGPT from random. This sample checks that B6 runs end to end;
    it is not evidence about ranking quality. A larger sample is needed (see timeline).
  * TREC 2021, both conditions: 41 exclusion-criterion predictions came back malformed (one trial,
    NCT04725812, where Qwen returned nested objects instead of [reason, ids, label]); they are
    skipped by the scorer. TREC 2022: 1 of 22 outputs unparsable.

8. CHALLENGES AND MITIGATIONS
==========================================================================================
  1. GPU capacity. Qwen3-32B does not fit; moved to Qwen3-8B, then to 8-bit on one 12 GB GPU
     because nodes are busy. Same model and settings for every system, so comparisons stay fair.
  2. Speed. vLLM could not be installed (PyPI too slow), so generation uses transformers at ~9
     tokens/s; one TrialGPT pair takes ~1-2 minutes, so all 20,529 dev pairs would take weeks.
     -> fixed, seeded samples of patients and trials; larger sample planned.
  3. Only one GPU available to the account (others draining or reserved). B7 needs the checker
     (Qwen) and the doctor (Llama) at once (~18 GB in 8-bit). -> alternate the two models in
     rounds on one GPU, or run the doctor as a separate job if a teammate has a GPU.
  4. Doctor gave unsupported "sure" answers. -> enforced quote and topic checks in code (section 5).
  5. Memorisation of public notes. -> T14 probe; no signal found (section 4).
  6. Leakage. The checker prompt included patient and trial IDs (fix requested); one smoke-test
     patient is now in the test split (documented, nothing tuned on it).
  7. Malformed model outputs crash parsing. -> parsers skip and count them; counts reported.
  8. Checker cost: 5 samples per rule are nearly identical under greedy decoding.
     -> proposal: 1 sample per rule (5x cheaper), pending team decision.
