Research prototype for the LMA course project. It replays answers recorded from the real system on three development patients (TREC 2021 / SIGIR 2016 cohorts) and is not for clinical use. How it works

Meet TrialGuide

An AI copilot that checks a patient against every rule of every candidate trial, and when the note is silent, asks the doctor instead of guessing.

1 · Pick a patientGrey, blurred sentences are the parts of the note the AI cannot see.
2 · Answer as the doctorYou see the whole record. Answer at most 3 questions, and say how sure you are.
3 · Watch the trials moveEvery answer settles rules and re-ranks the trials. Click a trial to see why.
